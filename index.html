About me

From marketing questions to data answers

I've always been drawn to the "why" behind what people choose. That curiosity took me from studying economics and communications at Wake Forest to an analytics-focused graduate program in Integrated Marketing Communication at Northwestern, and into a career built on marketing data.

Today I'm a Data Manager on the Marketing Operations team at tms. Our team supports the McDonald's Digital Menu Boards program, where my job is to make sure the right content shows up on the right restaurant menu boards. I design database systems and data workflows, build AI-powered automation in Airtable, work with analytics, IT and business development teams, and manage direct reports.

I'm now pursuing an MS in Artificial Intelligence with a Data Analytics specialization at Indiana Wesleyan University. My goal is to take the next step from reporting on marketing to building intelligent tools that personalize it, and to help teams use AI responsibly and practically.
